capital allocation framework
01
Tranche 1 — Fixed obligations are always funded first. Rent savings, debt repayment, transport, utilities. These never compete with investment decisions. Non-negotiable.
02
Tranche 2 — War Chest. All surplus after Tranche 1 and fixed savings flows here. The war chest is parked in T-bills or money market funds earning yield. It is not lazy — it is positioned. Capital is only deployed when a qualified opportunity is identified.
03
Tranche 3 — Deployed Capital. Active positions in equities, real estate accumulation, ThinkATS equity, and other asset classes. Each position has a written thesis, a hold condition, and a defined exit trigger.
04
DCA runs as a parallel system. Fixed monthly amounts into highest-conviction long positions (currently Access Bank). DCA is non-discretionary — it executes regardless of market mood.
05
Crypto is capped at 10% of total invested capital. BTC and ETH only. Deliberately de-risked. Not a speculation vehicle — a small asymmetric allocation.
06
No single equity may exceed 40% of the equities tranche at entry. Concentration is permitted after compounding but not at entry.
07
FX peg assumption: ₦1,600/$. All dollar-denominated targets are stress-tested at ₦1,700/$ before deployment approval.
